# Jazz Harris

**Product Ownership · Business Analysis · AI & Automation · Agile Delivery**

I'm an IT Business Analyst II at Coca-Cola Consolidated, the largest U.S. Coca-Cola bottler (17,000+ employees across 14 states, ~60M consumers), where I lead requirements definition and user-story creation for an Agile scrum team. I started as a full-stack developer and grew into the product side, so I work comfortably in both: writing the requirements and building the thing. Lately that's meant a lot of AI and automation — I built a Copilot Studio agent wired into our Azure DevOps board that creates and updates epics, features, and user stories, pulls board reports on demand, and drafts requirements and documentation. Outside of work I design and build web apps and tools, most recently Valora, a personal finance platform.
